Research & Clinical

MED-EL Launches First Totally Implantable Cochlear Implant in Europe

MED-EL launched TICI UNICO, the first totally implantable cochlear implant in the world, on the European market on September 29, 2026. The TICI contains internal components including a magnet, antenna coil, electronics, electrode array, implanted rechargeable battery, and microphone. In 2026, remote fitting technologies like HearCare enable cochlear implant audio processor fitting from the comfort of home.

Cochlear Implant Market Grows Significantly Through 2030

The cochlear implant market grew from $1.96 billion in 2025 to $2.13 billion in 2026 at an 8.7% CAGR. Growth drivers include increasing demand for personalized hearing solutions, rising adoption of smart and connected implants, expansion of early intervention hearing programs, and continuous innovation in sound processing technologies.

Updated Tinnitus Clinical Practice Guideline Released

The guideline covers hearing aids, cochlear implant evaluation, sound enrichment, cognitive behavioral therapy, and follow-up strategies. The guideline recommends against routinely using medications, dietary supplements, or repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation to treat tinnitus. New statements address self-harm, somatosensory tinnitus, cochlear implantation, and bimodal neuromodulation.
