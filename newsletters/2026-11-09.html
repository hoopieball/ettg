Research & Clinical

World's First Fully Implantable Cochlear Implant Now in Clinical Use

MED-EL's fully implantable TICI UNICO, with all components including the microphone located under the skin, has been fitted to patients in Vienna and St. Pölten outside the context of trials as of September 29, 2026. The world's first fully implantable cochlear implant is not visible from the outside and enables round-the-clock hearing tailored to the individual's hearing situation.

Optical Cochlear Implants Show Superior Neural Response

Research published in EMBO Molecular Medicine found that optical stimulation produced more distinct patterns of auditory neural activity than electrical stimulation, while requiring relatively small amounts of light energy. The findings suggest advances in light-sensitive proteins and implant design may address power limitations in optical cochlear implant research, illustrating how advances in gene delivery and molecular engineering are converging in experimental hearing restoration.

Clinical Practice Updates

Smartphone-Based Tinnitus Therapy Advances

Newcastle University researchers have unveiled a sound therapy that reduces tinnitus and has the potential to be delivered entirely online using everyday devices like smartphones. In an online trial with 77 people, the reduction in tinnitus lasted for at least three weeks after therapy ended, suggesting long-term benefits.

Auditory Processing Disorder: Role of Audiologists in Diagnosis

Audiologists are uniquely trained to administer specific diagnostic tests to diagnose APD, with tests designed to assess the maturity of auditory neural pathways with performance compared to age-based normative data. Treatment for APD consists of skills training to reorganize and improve the way the brain processes sound, as well as interventions and accommodations in the classroom, workplace, and at home.
