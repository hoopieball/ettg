Industry & Product News

Lineage Partners With William Demant to Advance Hearing Loss Gene Therapy

Lineage has partnered with William Demant Invest to advance ReSonance™ (ANP1), a cell therapy for hearing loss, through a three-year collaboration focusing on auditory neuronal cell transplant programs. Preclinical development will include cell manufacturing, proof-of-concept studies, translational models, and regulatory strategy to support a potential IND/CTA filing.

Signia Launches World's First Rechargeable Custom CIC with Directional Mics

The Insio Charge&Go CIC IX delivers up to 35 hours of battery life and 5X the speech enhancement in noise compared to other single-microphone CIC hearing aids. The device combines discretion with advanced directional microphone technology for improved speech understanding.

Advanced Circuit Sealing Extends Hearing Aid Durability in 2026

Internal components are now sealed using nanotechnology and plasma coatings that repel liquids at a molecular level. With components now shielded, faults caused by internal corrosion have dropped dramatically, and better-protected hearing aids maintain sound quality intact for longer.

Advanced Bionics Invests in Drug-Delivery Tech for Next-Gen Cochlear Implants

Advanced Bionics announced an equity investment and research collaboration with Spiral Therapeutics, bringing together three decades of cochlear implant expertise with Spiral's pioneering work in precision drug delivery for the inner ear. Cochlear Implant Pioneers Honored With 2026 Merkin Prize

Five pioneering researchers—Graeme Clark, Erwin Hochmair, Ingeborg Hochmair, Michael Merzenich, and Blake Wilson—have been jointly awarded the 2026 Richard N. Merkin Prize in Biomedical Technology for developing the modern cochlear implant. The award recognizes decades of innovation that transformed hearing restoration.

American Tinnitus Association Funds $655K in Next-Generation Research

The ATA invested more than $655,000 in next-generation tinnitus research on June 17, 2026. The funding reflects growing commitment to understanding why tinnitus affects some individuals more severely and to developing effective treatments.

American Tinnitus Association

Personalized Tinnitus Care Gains Recognition in 2026 Clinical Practice

Tinnitus is not a single condition but exists in several subtypes, each involving different mechanisms within the auditory and nervous systems. Patients with tinnitus connected to hearing loss may benefit significantly from hearing aid technology and sound stimulation, while others may require management protocols focusing on muscle tension relief, sleep regulation, and stress management.

In 2026, cochlear implant audio processor fitting no longer requires a clinic visit, with apps like HearCare enabling remote fitting from home. This advancement improves access for patients in underserved areas and reduces burden on specialized clinics.
