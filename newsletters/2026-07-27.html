Oticon Zeal Launches in US Market

Oticon Inc has launched Oticon Zeal, a new hearing aid style featuring an in-the-ear design that incorporates receiver-in-the-ear technology with rechargeability and modern connectivity.

AI-Powered Sound Processing Drives 2026 Innovation

New devices powered by NeuroFlow AI 2.0 platform provide approximately 24% improvement in speech enhancement and up to 20 dB of noise reduction. AI-powered sound processing automatically adjusts in real-time for clearer speech in noise, wind reduction, and natural music streaming.

Nuance Audio Glasses Gain FDA Clearance

EssilorLuxottica has received clearance from the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for its OTC Nuance Audio Glasses. The glasses function as an OTC hearing aid for consumers with mild-to-moderate hearing loss and use beamforming technology to enhance conversation clarity in noisy environments.

Advanced Bionics Invests in Cochlear Implant Innovation Partnership

Advanced Bionics has announced an equity investment and research collaboration with Spiral Therapeutics, combining AB's three decades of cochlear implant expertise with Spiral's precision drug-delivery technologies for the inner ear to explore novel drug-delivery methods supporting AB's long-term innovation roadmap.

Advanced Bionics

2026 Merkin Prize Honors Cochlear Implant Pioneers

Five pioneers—Graeme Clark, Erwin Hochmair, Ingeborg Hochmair, Michael Merzenich, and Blake Wilson—have been jointly awarded the 2026 Richard N. Merkin Prize in Biomedical Technology for developing the modern cochlear implant. This recognition celebrates decades of innovation improving hearing restoration.

PRNewswire

Recent APD Research Highlights Standardization Needs

A systematic review analyzing APD research developments from 2011 to 2025 examined diagnostic criteria, assessment protocols, and treatment effectiveness. The analysis found that among 156 studies, rehabilitative approaches (52 studies) dominated over medical interventions (4 studies), with cognitive skill enhancement comprising 52.8% of intervention strategies.

NCBI/PMC

CI Speech Perception in Noise Studies Underway

University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign is conducting a trial assessing methods to improve cochlear implant users' speech perception in noisy environments while maintaining sound localization abilities, with completion estimated August 2026.

Clinical & Community

Audiology Scope of Practice Modernization Continues

State audiology scope of practice modernization efforts are grounded in the urgent need to update state statutes to explicitly authorize audiologists to perform services such as vestibular testing and rehabilitation, cerumen removal, conducting health screenings, programming surgically implanted devices, and prescribing hearing aids.

Academy of Doctors of Audiology

Early Vestibular Dysfunction Identification in Pediatric Hearing Loss

Children with hearing loss face a significantly higher risk of vestibular dysfunction; however, early identification and intervention can dramatically improve outcomes. This underscores the importance of comprehensive pediatric audiology assessment.
