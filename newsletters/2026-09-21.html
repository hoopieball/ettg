Oticon Reveal: Dual AI Platform Transforms Hearing Aid Design

Oticon launched the Oticon Reveal hearing aid featuring a Dual AI system that concurrently enhances speech and ambient sounds, unlike competitor devices that prioritize speech by suppressing other sounds. The device integrates a Speech AI that continuously identifies and enhances speech signals from people in the user's environment, and a Context AI that recognizes and balances surrounding ambient and contextual sounds.

Hearing Health Foundation

Critical Appraisal of APD Diagnosis Released

Auditory Processing Disorder (APD) refers to listening difficulties in individuals with normal hearing thresholds but impaired processing of auditory information. Despite decades of research, its diagnostic validity in childhood remains highly debated. A new peer-reviewed narrative review examines diagnostic frameworks and interdisciplinary assessment approaches for improved clinical practice.

Frontiers in Human Neuroscience

Bimodal Neuromodulation Advances Tinnitus Treatment Options

Tinnitus is not a single condition, but instead exists in several subtypes, each involving different mechanisms within the auditory and nervous systems. Bimodal neuromodulation combines auditory stimulation (through customized sound therapy) with mild electrical stimulation (typically delivered to the tongue) to help retrain the brain's auditory pathways and reduce the brain's overactive response to tinnitus signals.
