October 19, 2026

As hearing aid technology accelerates and cochlear implant research expands, this week brought major developments in AI-powered speech processing, landmark regulatory approvals for tinnitus therapy, and continued innovation in teleaudiology access.

Industry & Product News

Demant Raises 2026 Outlook on Hearing Aid Revenue Surge

Demant's Q3 hearing aid revenue grew 17% organically, supported by Oticon Reveal and Zeal, prompting the company to raise its 2026 financial outlook. The strong performance underscores continued market momentum in premium hearing aid platforms.

Phonak Launches Roger On Sphere with AI Speech Separation

Phonak's Roger On Sphere adds AI speech separation to its remote microphone lineup, with a 3 dB SNR improvement. This advancement expands real-time speech processing capabilities for users in challenging listening environments.

Hearing Tracker

hear.com Receives Global Recognition for Teleaudiology Innovation

hear.com received a 2026 Global Recognition Award for its Clinic-in-a-Box® teleaudiology platform and Horizon IX hearing aids for expanding access to personalized, medical-grade hearing care. The honor reflects growing industry recognition of remote audiology delivery models.

Manila Times / Global Newswire

Fortell AI Hearing Aids Expand to 30+ States

Fortell has expanded its prescription AI hearing aids to more than 30 states through new brick-and-mortar clinics and its remote teleaudiology platform, Fortell Anywhere. In clinical testing, Fortell users understood 10 times more words in the loudest environments than wearers of other AI hearing aids.

Hearing Review

Oticon Zeal Wins 2026 Hearing Technology Innovator Award

Oticon earned Innovator of the Year for its Zeal hearing aid. As the industry's first hearing aid to create the completely new NXT in-the-Ear category and bring full functionality into an exceptionally discreet design, Oticon Zeal has the potential to reach people who may have previously avoided treatment.

Research & Clinical

MED-EL Launches World's First Totally Implantable Cochlear Implant in Europe

On September 29, 2026, MED-EL became the first company to launch TICI UNICO, the first totally implantable cochlear implant, on the European market. The TICI includes an implanted rechargeable battery and microphone, eliminating the need for external components.

Wikipedia

Cochlear Completes Recruitment for Fully Implantable Hearing Technology Trials

Cochlear announced completion of recruitment for its worldwide pivotal trial program evaluating fully implantable hearing technology, with patients experiencing more than 1 million hours of device use to date. This reflects the most comprehensive research programme focused on fully implantable hearing technology.

Cochlear Limited

UK LUCIA Trial to Assess Bilateral Cochlear Implants in Deaf Adults

A major UK-wide clinical trial led by the University of Cambridge will explore bilateral cochlear implants to improve hearing, wellbeing, and quality of life of profoundly deaf adults. The trial is expected to begin recruiting patients in autumn 2026.

University of Birmingham

AAO-HNSF Includes Lenire Bimodal Neuromodulation in Tinnitus Guidelines

The American Academy of Otolaryngology – Head and Neck Surgery Foundation has added Lenire (bimodal neuromodulation) to its clinical practice guidelines for tinnitus, the first new treatment recommendation added since the guidelines were introduced in 2014.

Clinical & Community

Study Links Hearing Aid Use to Lower Mortality in Large Multinational Study

A study of more than 64,000 adults links hearing aid use to lower mortality, but does not prove that hearing aids extend life. The research adds to growing evidence of hearing aid benefits beyond auditory outcomes.

Hearing Tracker

GN and RNID Launch Auracast Installation Guide for Theaters

RNID and GN launched an Auracast guide for theaters covering installation, audio quality, staff training, and support for audiences with hearing loss. The resource aims to improve hearing aid accessibility at live entertainment venues.
