Industry & Product News

Oticon Reveal Launches Dual AI System for Precision Hearing

Oticon unveiled Oticon Reveal, a premium rechargeable hearing aid featuring two dedicated AI systems—one for speech enhancement and one for context recognition—paired with an upgraded connectivity platform. The Speech AI continuously identifies and enhances speech from people around the user, while the Context AI recognizes and balances surrounding sounds. Oticon reports the combination results in a 60% stronger brain response compared to prior approaches.

Cochlear Implant Pioneers Awarded 2026 Merkin Prize

Five scientists and engineers—Graeme Clark, Erwin Hochmair, Ingeborg Hochmair, Michael Merzenich, and Blake Wilson—have been jointly awarded the 2026 Richard N. Merkin Prize in Biomedical Technology for their roles in developing the modern cochlear implant. The prize, worth $400,000 and shared among the five recipients, is administered by the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard.

Advanced Bionics

Standardization Gaps in APD Diagnosis Drive New Research Priorities

A comprehensive analysis of APD research developments from 2011 to 2025 examined diagnostic criteria and treatment effectiveness. Among 156 studies analyzed, medical interventions were markedly underrepresented compared to rehabilitative approaches, with cognitive skill enhancement dominating intervention strategies at 52.8%. The substantial imbalance between medical and rehabilitative interventions necessitates standardized diagnostic protocols, with future research prioritizing large-scale controlled trials and unified diagnostic criteria development.

Frontiers in Human Neuroscience

2026 Tinnitus Research Emphasizes Precision Medicine and Personalization

A precision-medicine framework for tinnitus integrates vascular diagnostics, personalized sound therapy, emotional regulation, and aging research, with accurate stratification determining whether tinnitus becomes persistent or remains clinically manageable. Across sound therapy, neuromodulation concepts, environmental mapping, and data-driven behavioral models, tinnitus is recognized as a multi-layered interaction between the ear, brain, attention, memory, emotion, and surrounding stimuli.

Audiology-Fitted Hearing Aids Outperform OTC in Real-World Study

Patients with audiologist-fitted hearing aids experienced significantly better outcomes than those using pre-set over-the-counter (OTC) hearing aids, with or without telecare. This finding highlights the continued value of professional audiology practice in optimizing patient outcomes, even as OTC market expands.

Gene Therapy Restores Hearing Within Weeks in Hereditary Deafness

Gene therapy can improve hearing in children and adults with congenital deafness or severe hearing impairment, with a new gene therapy giving people born deaf the chance to hear, often within just weeks. This breakthrough opens new frontiers for audiology professionals advising families on emerging treatment options for inherited hearing loss.
