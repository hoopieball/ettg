Ear to the Ground

October 26, 2026

This week brings landmark guideline updates, breakthrough implant technologies, and significant scope-of-practice advances reshaping the audiology landscape. From invisible cochlear implants reaching commercial availability to FDA-backed tinnitus treatments gaining formal endorsement, the field continues to expand its clinical reach and treatment options.

Industry & Product News

Lenire Added to AAO Tinnitus Clinical Practice Guidelines

The American Academy of Otolaryngology has included Lenire as a treatment option in the Academy's new Clinical Practice Guidelines for Tinnitus, marking the first new treatment added since the guidelines' establishment. This FDA-approved bimodal neuromodulation device gains significant credibility within mainstream clinical recommendations, giving patients and providers an evidence-backed tool for tinnitus management.
Source: Audiology Online

First Totally Implantable Cochlear Implant Launches in Europe

The first commercially available invisible cochlear implant—MED-EL's TICI UNICO—received CE mark approval and completed first commercial surgeries on September 29, 2026. The rechargeable device provides hearing performance comparable to conventional cochlear implants, though larger studies are needed to assess long-term outcomes.
Source: MED-EL Blog

Oticon Zeal Wins 2026 Innovator of the Year

As the industry's first hearing aid to create the completely new NXT in-the-Ear category with full functionality in an exceptionally discreet design, Oticon Zeal empowers users who don't want to compromise between discreetness and performance. Multiple hearing technology innovator awards also recognized MED-EL's DualSync bimodal technology and Sonnet 3 processor.
Source: Hearing Review

Ontario Advances Over-the-Counter Hearing Aid Access

Ontario's October 2026 announcement includes legislative and regulatory changes to enable access to qualifying Health Canada-approved hearing aids without a prescription. The expansion addresses access while raising clinical considerations about patient self-diagnosis and education.
Source: Hearing Health Matters

Hear.com Wins Global Innovation Award for Teleaudiology Platform

The Miami-based company earned recognition for its Clinic-in-a-Box teleaudiology platform and Horizon IX hearing aids that address speech clarity in noise while expanding access to personalized, medical-grade hearing care.
Source: Hearing Review

Research & Clinical

Light-Based Cochlear Implants Show Frequency Selectivity Promise

Researchers demonstrated that experimental light-based cochlear implants can activate auditory neurons with greater frequency selectivity than conventional electrical stimulation, with findings published October 22, 2026, in EMBO Molecular Medicine. The research combined a light-sensitive protein with experimental optical cochlear implants containing up to 10 miniature light-emitting diodes.
Source: Hearing Health Matters

Cochlear Completes Recruitment for Fully Implantable Hearing Technology Trial

Patients across Cochlear's fully implantable hearing system research programmes have experienced more than 1 million hours with their devices, reflecting the most comprehensive research programme focused on this new category. Recruitment completed in the ELEVATE study across 11 US sites and the ENTERPRISE study across five European and Australian sites.
Source: Cochlear

AAO-HNS Publishes First Tinnitus Guideline Update Since 2014

The 2026 guideline requires a full hearing evaluation for all tinnitus patients and backs hearing aids, cognitive behavioral therapy, and bimodal neuromodulation as treatment options. The guidelines advise against routine medications, supplements, and magnetic stimulation for tinnitus itself.
Source: Utah Hearing Aids

American Tinnitus Association Announces Record Research Investment

In FY26, ATA made the largest research investment in its 55-year history, awarding more than $655,000 to five researchers. The robust funding supports the organization's commitment to advancing treatment development and clinical outcomes for tinnitus patients.
Source: American Tinnitus Association

Clinical & Community

APSO Opens Public Comment on National Audiology Assistant Standard

The Audiology Practice Standards Organization is accepting public review of its proposed Audiology Assistant Standard from October 26–November 6, 2026, encouraging audiologists to submit feedback before the standard moves to final draft. This effort addresses the growing need for workforce consistency as audiology practices increasingly use assistants for support roles.
Source: Hearing Health Matters

Arkansas Finalizes Audiology Scope Expansion Rules

On August 5, 2026, Arkansas finalized rules implementing Act 517 of 2025, expanding audiology scope to include cerumen removal, ordering radiographic imaging, and fitting cochlear implant processors. The expansion reflects ongoing legislative momentum to modernize state-level audiology practice acts across the nation.
Source: American Academy of Audiology
