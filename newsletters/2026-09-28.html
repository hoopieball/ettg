Industry & Product News

Signia Launches Multi-Adaptive AI Platform

Signia has introduced Multi-adaptive Xperience (MaX), the world's first multi-dimensional AI hearing aid platform with 4 DNNs, supporting wearers in noisy, vibrant environments. This breakthrough represents a significant leap forward in AI-driven sound processing for real-world listening challenges.

Starkey Omega AI Wins Healthcare Innovation Award

Starkey announced that its Omega AI has been named a winner in the Best Use of Artificial Intelligence category at the 2026 Healthcare Eagles Awards. The recognition highlights industry momentum toward machine learning-driven hearing device innovation.

Signia Advances with Compact Ready-to-Wear Design

The Active Mini IX combines premium speech enhancement technology with a compact, ready-to-wear design that enables same-day fittings without the need for custom ear molds. As the world's smallest ready-to-wear in-the-ear hearing aid with connectivity, it offers a nearly invisible hearing solution without compromising performance.

Advanced Bionics Collaborates on Drug-Delivery Innovation

Advanced Bionics announced an equity investment and research collaboration with Spiral Therapeutics. This collaboration brings together Advanced Bionics' three decades of expertise in cochlear implant innovation with Spiral Therapeutics' pioneering work in precision drug delivery technologies for the inner ear, with the effort aiming to explore how novel drug-delivery methods could support AB's long-term innovation roadmap.

Cochlear Implant Testing Battery Improves Consistency

The Minimum Speech Test Battery–Version 3, released in 2024, can help determine cochlear implant candidacy and assess cochlear implant performance. In the 2 years since its development, this test battery has led to increased consistency in both evaluation and follow-up care.

ASHA Journals Academy

Bimodal Neuromodulation Shows Promise for Tinnitus

In a 2022 study of 191 people with mostly moderate to severe tinnitus, 70 percent of those who used Lenire for an hour a day for 12 weeks reported a benefit. By the end of the 12-week study, 66 percent rated their tinnitus as mild or slight. This FDA-approved approach combines sound therapy with targeted tongue stimulation.

AARP

Multidisciplinary Approach Advances Tinnitus Management

Available treatment options for tinnitus include counselling, cognitive-behavioral therapy, hearing aids and cochlear implants for patients with relevant hearing loss, sound generators, novel auditory stimulation approaches, tinnitus retraining therapy, pharmacological treatment, neurofeedback, brain stimulation, and digital approaches. Clinicians are increasingly recognizing the need for coordinated care across disciplines.

Ototoxicity Monitoring Guidance for Teprotumumab Users

Audiologists should implement proactive baseline and monitoring audiologic assessments for patients prescribed teprotumumab, as ototoxicity risk is well-documented; this review provides updated clinical guidance on assessment and management strategies.

Hearing Tracker / NeuroAudiology Newsletter

Interdisciplinary Collaboration for Auditory Processing Assessment

Diagnosis is made by the audiologist following completion of a battery of tests, performed in conjunction with a complete speech, language and auditory processing evaluation from a speech-language pathologist, who then determines the need for therapeutic intervention. This collaborative model improves detection and management of central auditory disorders.
