Industry & Product News

Oticon Launches Reveal: Industry's First Dual AI Hearing Aid

Oticon's new Reveal platform represents the company's newest platform-level introduction, built on a distinct AI architecture rather than an incremental update to an existing line. The Speech AI continuously identifies and enhances speech from people around the user, while the Context AI recognizes and balances surrounding sounds, with the company reporting a 60% stronger brain response compared to prior approaches. Oticon Reveal will be available to order starting Aug 24, 2026.

Legato Emerges With $12M in Funding for AI Hearing Glasses

A new hearing tech startup is emerging from stealth with $12 million in funding and a first look at its AI hearing glasses. Legato Frames integrate the company's patented hearing-assistance technology into eyewear arms and are expected to launch later this fall. The company addresses the treatment gap, as hearing loss affects an estimated 50 million adults in the United States, but only around 20% of those with diagnosed hearing loss seek treatment.

Lineage Partners with William Demant on Cell Therapy for Hearing Loss

Lineage and William Demant Invest announced a three-year collaboration to advance Lineage's auditory neuronal cell transplant program, ReSonance™ (ANP1), for the treatment of hearing loss. Preclinical development will include cell manufacturing, proof-of-concept studies, translational and functional models, delivery methods, outcome measures, regulatory strategy, and market analysis.

Research & Clinical

Brain Research Reveals Serotonin May Worsen Tinnitus

Scientists have uncovered evidence that serotonin — the same brain chemical boosted by many antidepressants — may actually worsen tinnitus. This finding from advanced light-based brain stimulation research in mice challenges conventional understanding and may inform future pharmacological approaches to tinnitus management.

Comprehensive Review: Tinnitus Distress is Highly Treatable

A comprehensive review of 132 intervention studies covering CBT, digital therapies, hearing technologies, neuromodulation, pharmacology, surgery, and multimodal care shows that tinnitus distress is highly treatable, particularly through CBT and integrated care pathways. The findings support multimodal, personalized approaches to patient care.

New Analysis: APD Needs Standardized Diagnostic Protocols

A recent analysis of APD research developments (2011–2025) examining diagnostic criteria and treatment found that rehabilitative approaches dominate (n = 52) with cognitive skill enhancement and computer-based rehabilitation comprising most interventions (52.8% and 26.4%, respectively). The substantial imbalance between medical and rehabilitative interventions necessitates standardized diagnostic protocols and enhanced multidisciplinary collaboration.

WSA and ADA Launch "Listen Up" Awareness Campaign

WSA and the Academy of Doctors of Audiology (ADA) announced a national partnership to launch Listen Up, a public awareness and Public Service Announcement (PSA) initiative designed to encourage adults 50 and older to make hearing screening a routine part of their healthcare program.
